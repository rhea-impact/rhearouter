❯ could it run 8b ai models 

● Yes, your computer can run 8B AI models, though with some considerations:

  GPU Inference (Recommended)

  With your NVIDIA RTX A2000 8GB, you can run 8B models using quantization:

  - INT4 quantization (~4-5 GB VRAM): Fast, good quality
  - INT8 quantization (~8 GB VRAM): Better quality, will use most of your VRAM
  - FP16 full precision (~16 GB VRAM): ❌ Won't fit in 8GB VRAM

  Popular frameworks like llama.cpp, Ollama, vLLM, or text-generation-webui all
  support quantized models.

  CPU Inference (Alternative)

  With your 32 GB RAM and 16-core i9-12950HX, you can also run 8B models on CPU:
  - Slower than GPU but perfectly functional
  - Can use higher precision since you have plenty of RAM
  - Good for when VRAM is constrained

  Practical Examples

  Models that will run well:
  - Llama 3.1 8B (quantized)
  - Mistral 7B-8B (quantized)
  - Gemma 2 9B (quantized)
  - Qwen 2.5 7B (quantized)

  Recommended Setup

  For best performance, use Ollama or llama.cpp with Q4 or Q5 quantization -
  you'll get good inference speed (5-15 tokens/sec) with decent quality on your
  hardware.

  Your system is well-suited for running 8B models locally for development and
  experimentation.
